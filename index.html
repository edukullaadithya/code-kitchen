Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about searching, listing, and moving with RentRight.

How does the AI recommendation score calculate property matches?

Our recommendation engine evaluates listings across a 5-dimension vector: Location Relevance (40%), Budget Elasticity (20%), Safety Index (15%), Commute Duration (15%), and Amenity Match (10%) to produce an objective, personalized match score from 0 to 100.

How can flat owners and administrators publish new listings?

Flat sellers and property managers can log in via the Administrator portal, click "💼 Admin Dashboard", and submit flat details with pricing, photos, and safety attributes. The listing instantly becomes visible to thousands of verified renters.

Can I search for specific properties or landmarks by name?

Yes! You can enter locality names (e.g. Kukatpally, Madhapur), compound queries (e.g. "Kukatpally, Hyderabad"), PIN codes, or exact property names like "HKS Luxury Flats" and "xyz hotel" to discover matching properties instantly.

Is my search preference and contact data private?

Absolutely. Your preferences, search history, and inquiry credentials are encrypted with enterprise-grade security and are only shared with authorized property owners when you explicitly express interest.
